0. Confirm every position has its GTC stop sell (place any missing one).
1. Check fills and pending orders from the trade run; cancel stale unfilled limits or
   re-price them if the thesis still holds.
2. Review every position against its invalidation and target. Sell what's broken; trim
   what's hit target. Use any remaining settled buying power only for high-conviction
   names already on today's plan.
3. Remember T+1: proceeds from a sale today are NOT spendable until tomorrow. Do not try.
4. Write the end-of-day journal entry: P&L (`get_realized_pnl`, positions), what the
   market did to the thesis, and at least one lesson in `kb/lessons.md` if there is one.
